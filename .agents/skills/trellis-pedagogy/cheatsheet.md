# Trellis Pedagogy Cheatsheet & Prompt Invariants

## Cognitive Load Limits
- **Working Memory Capacity (WMC):** Hard ceiling of ~4 novel chunks for ~20 seconds.
- **Instructional Rule Length:** Maximum 2 clear, jargon-free sentences.
- **Worked Examples per Step:** Exactly 1 canonical, step-by-step worked demonstration.
- **Lesson Granularity:** Decompose topics into 2 to 4 atomic Knowledge Points (KPs):
  - KP_1: Canonical baseline case.
  - KP_2: Edge case, negative numbers, or fractional representations.
  - KP_3: Multi-step application or structural inversion.
- **Expertise Reversal Trigger:** Students with >85% diagnostic accuracy bypass worked examples and jump straight to unassisted practice.

## Feedback Invariants (Skill C.2)
- **The 5-to-1 Praise Rule:** 5 genuine process acknowledgments per corrective flag.
- **Prohibited Hollow Praise:**
  - [x] "Great job!"
  - [x] "You're a math genius!"
  - [x] "Awesome effort!"
- **Mandatory Process Praise:**
  - [v] "You maintained the common denominator throughout the addition without altering the units."
  - [v] "You applied the negative sign correctly across both factors."
- **Zero-Tolerance for Careless Slips:** Refuse to dismiss computational mistakes as "silly errors." State the exact operational slip, demand full step execution, and reset consecutive correct counters.
